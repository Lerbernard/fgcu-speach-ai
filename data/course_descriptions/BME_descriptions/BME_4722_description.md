# BME 4722 — Course Description

Subject: BME | Course: BME 4722

BME 4722
 Health Care Engineering - 3 credits(s)
 Apply the engineering design method and operations management concepts to solve problems pertinent to health care industries, e.g. hospitals, clinics, and research labs, as well as problems related to design development in industry. Specific topics include methods of problem solving, data gathering, statistical methods of data analysis, and developing solutions.
 Prerequisite(s)
 STA 2023 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C or STA 2037 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
