# BME 3506C — Course Description

Subject: BME | Course: BME 3506C

BME 3506C
 Circuits for Bioengineers - 3 credits(s)
 Principles of circuit design, simulation, and analysis; DC and AC circuit theory; first and second order circuits, and principles of operational amplifiers.
 Prerequisite(s)
 ( PHY 2049C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C or ( PHY 2049 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C and PHY 2049L for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C ) ) and MAP 2302 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
