# CWR 6637 — Course Description

Subject: CWR | Course: CWR 6637

CWR 6637
 Storm Water Mgmt and Modeling - 3 credits(s)
 This course covers the chemical, physical, biological, and hydrologic aspects of rainfall-runoff and stormwater management. Topics include hydrologic processes, water chemistry, sediment transport, infrastructure materials, best management practices, and treatment/reuse systems. Students learn how constituent properties, loads, and modeling tools can support the design and evaluation of stormwater control, treatment, and reuse practices. Ecological and economic issues related to rainfall-runoff control are also discussed.
