# EES 4102C — Course Description

Subject: EES | Course: EES 4102C

EES 4102C
 Wastewater Microbiology - 3 credits(s)
 This course introduces students to the general concepts of environmental microbiology. There is a major emphasis on relevant lab techniques. Students will learn the basics of microbial growth, methods to quantify and identify microbes, and the ecological relevant of many environmental microbes.
 Prerequisite(s)
 ENV 3006C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
