# COP 1500 — Course Description

Subject: COP | Course: COP 1500

COP 1500
 Intro to Computer Science - 3 credits(s)
 Introducing basic concept of computing: discrete models, computer architecture and organization, operating systems, programming languages, software design and construction, performance evaluation, with a focus on programming.
 Prerequisite(s)
 MAC 1105 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C or MAC 1147 or MAC 2157 or MAC 2311 or Accuplacer Coll. Math Subscore 066
