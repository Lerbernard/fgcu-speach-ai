# BCN 3240C — Course Description

Subject: BCN | Course: BCN 3240C

BCN 3240C
 Construction Methods and Equip - 3 credits(s)
 Basic understanding of construction equipment and methods employed in different sectors: building; heavy; industrial; and utility construction. Factors affecting the selection of equipment, determination of ownership and operating costs, estimating earthwork quantities, calculation of equipment and fleet production, equipment and quality control in paving operations, and information resources pertinent to equipment management.
 Prerequisite(s)
 BCN 3218C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
