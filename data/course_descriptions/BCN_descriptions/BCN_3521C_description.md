# BCN 3521C — Course Description

Subject: BCN | Course: BCN 3521C

BCN 3521C
 Electrical Systems - 3 credits(s)
 Principles and practices of electrical and lighting systems used in residential, commercial and industrial applications. Emphasis is placed on coordination of the National Electrical Code (NEC), design, and installation of various systems in the building process.
 Prerequisite(s)
 ( ( PHY 2053 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C and PHY 2053L for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C ) or ( PHY 2053 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C and PHY 2048L for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C ) or PHY 2053C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C ) and BCN 4612C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
