# BCN 3611C — Course Description

Subject: BCN | Course: BCN 3611C

BCN 3611C
 Construction Estimating I - 3 credits(s)
 Classification of work, quantity survey techniques and basic estimating principles applied to simple construction projects.
 Prerequisite(s)
 BCN 1210C for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C and BCN 3255 for level Undergraduate with minimum grade of C
