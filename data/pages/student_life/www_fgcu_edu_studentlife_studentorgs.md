How you spend your time outside the classroom is an integral part of the college experience, and participating in an organization will help prepare you to live in an engaged society.

  * Are you interested in serving as an advisor to a Registered Student Organization?
    * Eligibility:
      * A Florida Gulf Coast University full-time faculty or staff member
      * May advise an organization outside of field of study or discipline
      * May advise more than one organization
      * Be able to store RSOs equipment and supplies purchases in their campus office
      * Complete Advisor Acknowledgement Form
      * Is encouraged to attend RSO and Advisor Trainings
