## PROGRAMS

Prevention & Wellness

Prevention & Wellness provides students with health education and resources to them improve their overall well-being.

Fitness

Free group fitness classes are offered daily for all skill levels. Personal training is available to students, faculty and staff.

Intramural Sports

Participate in organized competitive or recreational sports regardless of your skill level. Open to students, faculty and staff.

RecVentures

Learn to sail at the Waterfront, go on exciting day trips or check out a bike using our Pedal Power Bike Share program.

Sport Clubs

Sport clubs are formed by students with a common interest in an activity. With over 30 clubs to choose from, you have the opportunity to represent FGCU against other universities.
