## Make an Appointment

In order to be seen by a healthcare provider or to be treated in our facility, please call Student Health Services at 239-590-7966.

If you need a physical, STD/STI, women's health, birth control, pregnancy test, or concussion evaluation appointment please call.

Students may schedule sick appointments such as cough, sore throat, and flu like symptoms through their Student Health Portal 24/7.

You may also come in and schedule an appointment that meets your needs.

Student Health Services is adjacent to Parking Garage 2, behind the Arts Complex.

If you need to make an immunization appointment, please call 239-590-1254.

Thank you!

**Reminder:**

* You must bring a valid picture ID with you for all appointments.
* Visit confirmations are only provided to students who are seen for an appointment on the day of their absence. Notes may excuse you for multiple days following a visit, at the discretion of the provider, if the illness or injury is severe but we are not able to backdate notes.
* Missed appointments not canceled at least 2 hours ahead of the appointment time are subject to the $25 no-show fee.
* Bring a form of payment for any nominal charges you may incur at the time of your appointment. Student Health Services takes all major forms of payment including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and Apple Pay.

## Class Absence Request Form

Unforeseen events or circumstances may occur that cause a student to be absent from class. These could include illness, medical issues, bereavement, accident, hospitalization, pregnancy and/or parenting, or a catastrophic event. Please use the link below to fill out the class absence notice request form from Student Care Services. Class absences are generally sent for situations where the student is out for five days or longer or whose condition does not allow the student to communicate directly with each professor in a timely manner. Please note that this form in not reviewed after-hours, on the weekends, or during university holidays or breaks. If you need to report an emergency, call the University Police Department at (239)590-1900, or dial 911.

* Insurance & Billing

  Health Insurance Information

  FGCU does not offer a domestic insurance plan. There is an international student plan that is coordinated by the Global Engagement Office (GEO). Please click this link to see the GEO website: here

  All visits to Student Health Services, regardless of insurance coverage, are free. Some medications, labs and supplies are provided at nominal prices. Students need to bring some form of payment to each appointment to cover the possibility of a charge.

  Billing Information

  **Check-out** **Information:** Not all visits incur charges; however, if your provider recommends labs or medications, there will be charges that are incurred at nominal rates. Any charge(s) incurred at Student Health Services must be paid for at the time of service in the form of credit card, debit card, or Apple pay. Please bring a form of payment for your visit.

  **No-Show Fee:**Students who incur the $25 fee can pay directly at Student Health Services. This will allow them to avoid the financial hold from Student Account Services. Students are granted 1 business week from time of charge to pay; failure to complete payment at Student Health will result in the charge being transferred to their FGCU account.

* Privacy Practices

  Our Pledge to You

  We understand that medical information about you is personal. We are committed to protecting medical information about you. Throughout this Notice, we will refer to your "protected health information" as your PHI.

  We create a record of the services you receive at FGCU SHS to provide quality care and to comply with certain legal requirements. This notice applies to all of the records, electronic or paper, of your care generated by SHS practitioners. We may not disclose any more of your PHI than is necessary.

  We are required by law:

  * To make sure that your PHI is kept private.
  * To inform you of our legal duties and your rights in regards to privacy practices with your PHI.
  * To follow the terms of the notice that currently is in effect.

  View our Student Handout Notice of Privacy Practices.

  HIPAA

  In 1996, the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) was endorsed by the US Congress as a result of the American public's concern about how patient health information was stored, accessed, disclosed and shared. The HIPAA Privacy Rule provided the first nationally-recognized and enforced set of regulations concerning the use and disclosure of what is commonly called “protected health information.”

  HIPAA requires all healthcare professionals and those with access to patient data to operate within strict standards to safeguard protected health information. The US Department of Health and Human Services enforces HIPAA law and imposes penalties on institutions that do not make good-faith efforts to ensure the privacy and security of protected health information.

  Student Health Services (SHS) operates in strict adherence to HIPAA laws and regulations. HIPAA requirements are incorporated into policies and procedures department-wide, and are a vital, required component of new employee and annual staff refresher training.

  Included in Notice of Privacy Practices is detailed information about how patients can:

  · Obtain a copy of their health information · Request SHS amend health information in their patient records · Receive an accounting of certain disclosures made of their health information · Request SHS restrict the use and disclosure of their health information · Request information regarding how SHS may contact them about medical matters · Receive notification of how SHS may use protected health information

  To read more about the Privacy Rule, including the regulatory background, who is covered by the rule, what is protected, and general principles for uses and disclosures of health information, see the OCR's Official Website. You may also want to read about HIPAA's Final Rule.

* Partnerships

  UBYou is a student wellness and success app that connects you to FGCU campus resources and self-care activities to support your well-being and success.

  * UBYou is the Student Wellness Success Hub designed for students by students. Download the application here: UBYou
  * The UBYou application offers a daily check in to reflect on your day as well as many physical and mental health exercises to privately support you. The application also connects you to a hub of resources from all across campus to make finding and utilizing resources easier.

  How does it work?

  College can be tough, but you don't have to face it alone. UBYou offers personalized support to help you conquer stress, boost productivity, and thrive academically!

  * UBYou is FREE for FGCU students. Use your FGCU student email when you signup.
  * Tailored support and guidance to assist your well-being and overall success.
  * Reflect on your day with UBYou's Wellness Check-In and track your progress.
  * Access the resources at FGCU that best suit your needs.

  How do i get started?

  You can access UBYou by clicking here. Set up your profile using your FGCU email credentials. After signing up, you will gain access to a hub and search engine of FGCU resources as well as a wide variety of meditations, affirmations, breathing exercises, workout demonstrations, educational how-to videos, and much more!

  UBYou created by an FGCU student, understands the ins and outs of navigating your campus. Signing up is easy. Access privately at any time.
