# Adaptive Services

## FGCU Adaptive Services
Adaptive Services strives to enhance access and opportunity for all students, faculty, staff and guests with disabilities by providing effective, reasonable accommodations and by educating the campus community on issues related to disabilities.

### How to Request Accommodations
* Prospective Students & Family Members

  **If you are a prospective student or family member**, please email us at adaptive@fgcu.edu , or call us at **239-590-7956** if you have any questions about our program. You can also find useful information under the Prospective Student tab.

  Adaptive Services Overview

  **Prospective Students**

  We are excited to work with you and we look forward to seeing you soon!
* First Time Registration for Incoming/Current Students

  **If you are an incoming student for the Fall 2026 semester, or you are a current student needing accommodations for the first time**, please email us at adaptive@fgcu.edu , or by phone at 239-590-7956, to get more information on scheduling an appointment.

  Learn more about **first time** accommodation requests for **Incoming & Current Students**

  We are excited to work with you and we look forward to seeing you soon!
* Students Currently Registered with Adaptive Services

  **If you are a student currently registered with us**, please refer to the Adaptive Services Canvas page for all information regarding receiving accommodations and services in-person and virtually.

  Learn more about accommodations for **Current Students**

  We are excited to work with you and we look forward to seeing you soon!
* Faculty & Staff

  **Employees of Florida Gulf Coast University can request reasonable accommodations and services related to a disability by contacting Adaptive Services and setting up an appointment.** Information on the process and required forms can be found in the Faculty/Staff Resource Guide.

  Faculty and Staff Accessibility Resource Guide

  Supervisors are required to refer any employee requesting assistance to Adaptive Services for determination of the reasonable accommodations and subsequent procedures.

  **Adaptive Services' Faculty Canvas Page**

  **Canvas Note:** Requires Google Chrome. For access to Adaptive Services in Canvas, please contact Adaptive Services.

Florida Gulf Coast University, under the guidelines of federal legislation, provides access to activities, programs and services to students, faculty, staff and guests with disabilities.
