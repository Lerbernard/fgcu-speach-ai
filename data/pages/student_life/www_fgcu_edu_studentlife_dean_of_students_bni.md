# Basic Needs Initiatives

## Creating Stability. Empowering Success.
Basic Needs Initiatives (BNI) offers personalized academic and personal support to students with lived experience in foster care, homelessness, or ward of the state status – helping them thrive at FGCU and beyond.

Our mission is to support students from the moment they arrive at FGCU through graduation and beyond—connecting them to the resources they need to thrive academically, personally, and professionally. We are committed to ensuring that no Eagle has to choose between life’s challenges and their education.

### Our Services

Academic & Career Support

* Academic and life skills workshops
* One-on-one mentoring and guidance
* Career and graduate school preparation

Financial & Resource Navigation

* Financial literacy and FAFSA guidance
* DCF/Homeless waiver assistance
* Referrals to campus and community resources

Student Advocacy

* Ongoing case management and student support
* Collaboration with campus partners to reduce barriers to success
