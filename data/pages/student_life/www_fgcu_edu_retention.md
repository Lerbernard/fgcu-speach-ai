# Retention & Graduation Services

## Supporting Success From Start to Finish

Retention & Graduation Services encompasses a range of support mechanisms and resources designed to help students persist in their academic studies, overcome challenges, and ultimately graduate from their programs. Our office aims to improve student success rates and enhance graduation outcomes by connecting students to the right campus and community resources at the right time.

### Transition Programs

Transition Programs at FGCU provides the gateway to making the most of your FGCU experience. Our programs are designed to help students navigate the academic, social, and personal transitions that come with entering college, ensuring they are well-prepared for the opportunities and responsibilities ahead.
