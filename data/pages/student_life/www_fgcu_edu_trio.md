# TRIO and Outreach Programs

## Dedicated support for Completing Your Academic Journey

The TRIO and Outreach Programs provide opportunities for students to advance their academic and personal growth, gain financial responsibility, explore careers and understand college requirements. Florida Gulf Coast University brings together 5 grant-based programs that help you prepare to succeed in college and stay on track to finishing a degree.
