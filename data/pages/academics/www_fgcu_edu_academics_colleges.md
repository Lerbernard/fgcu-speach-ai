### Our Schools

Bower School of Music & the Arts

The Bower School within the College of Arts & Sciences offers a first-class education in music performance and theory, music therapy, art and theatre. The Arts Complex and Music Building form the cultural heart of campus, drawing audiences from throughout Southwest Florida.

Daveler & Kauanui School of Entrepreneurship

You can make change happen in any field throughstrong leadership and investment in ventures or products. Our bachelor's and master's degrees prepare you to join thenext generation of innovators, visionaries and industry-shaping leaders who steer the ever-changing global marketplace.

School of Nursing

Our student-centered nursing education and strong clinical partnerships in Southwest Florida prepare you to provide the skilled and transformative care your patients need. Most of our programs deliver 100% graduate employment rates and excellent first-time certification pass rates.

School of Resort & Hospitality Management

What better place to study resort management than a vacation destination? With 100% placement in paid internships, our degree programs prepare you to take your place among the leaders in event, hotel and club management.

The Water School

Located amid Florida's complex freshwater and saltwater systems, FGCU is uniquely positioned to explore water-based issues. Collaborating with interdisciplinary faculty, you can study waterway health and research solutions benefiting our environment, public health and economy.
