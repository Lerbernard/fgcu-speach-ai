### Important Immigration Update for F-1 International Students & J-1 Scholars
The FGCU Global Engagement Office would like to inform F-1 international students and J-1 scholars of important changes to U.S. immigration regulations that are scheduled to take effect on **September 15, 2026**. Beginning on this date, students and scholars entering or reentering the United States will no longer be admitted under **Duration of Status (D/S)**. Instead, they will receive a **date-specific Admit Until Date (AUD)** on their Form I-94, which will determine how long they are authorized to remain in the United States.

These changes may affect:

* Program completion timelines
* Grace periods after program completion
* Extension of Stay (EOS) requirements

* Optional Practical Training (OPT) and STEM OPT
* International travel and reentry
* Certain academic program changes

**No action is required at this time.**

The FGCU Global Engagement Office is actively monitoring guidance from the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS), U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS), U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP). Additional information and guidance—including updates related to OPT, STEM OPT, travel, and Extension of Stay procedures—will be shared as it becomes available.

We encourage all F-1 students and J-1 scholars to monitor their FGCU email and this webpage for future updates. Thank you for your patience as we work to support our international community throughout this transition.

**Questions?** Please contact the **FGCU Global Engagement Office**.

* FGCU International Travel Registry FAQs

  **How long will it take for my international travel to be approved after I enter my authorization request?**

  * Please allow a minimum of 2 weeks for your approval to be processed once your supervisor has approved your request. This may be longer depending on what information is needed and the destination of travel.

  **What are the benefits of the travel registry?**

  * **Emergency Communications:**Your travel registration enables the FGCU Global Engagement Office to reach you in the event of an emergency abroad. The travel registry is the University’s official means for sharing urgent health, safety and security-related information.

  **Who is required to register travel?**

  * All FGCU faculty, staff and some students traveling abroad for official "Mission Critical" University related business.

  **Is this proposed activity considered mission critical (essential)?**

  Mission Critical Travel includes:

  * Travel to participate in, attend, or conduct conferences, workshops, conventions or professional association meetings that advance or pertain to FGCU's mission.
  * Travel to conduct scholarly activities, including performances, exhibitions, primary or secondary research on subjects and topics that advance or pertain to FGCU's mission.
  * Travel to conduct service activities that advance or pertain to the FGCU mission
  * Travel to conduct grant-related activities as allowed by the grant.

  **When should I register my travel?**

  * Travel should be registered at least 45 days prior to departure.
  * Travelers should update their registrations as additional information becomes available or if changes occur during travel.
  * If your travel plans should change or are canceled for any reason, please update the travel registry.

  **How will I know when my request has been approved?**

  * Look for important updates in your outlook for emails from noreply@abeona.com
  * Your final approval email will come from noreply@abeona.com with an approval statement in the subject line.
  * Travelers must have final approval completed before entering a Spend Authorization in workday.

  6. Register your trip in the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP).

  All FGCU faculty, staff and students traveling internationally on University Sponsored International Travel are required to be covered by the CISI Travel Abroad Insurance Plan.
