### Accepting Your Admission Decision

Now that you are an Eagle, review each section below to get off to a flying start.

* Claim your Eagle Access account to receive all future communications.

  The next step in your enrollment process is to claim your **Eagle Access account**. Eagle Access will allow you access to the FGCU email system, EagleMail. Please be advised, University offices and staff/faculty will be sending important information to this address and may not respond to communication from personal email accounts. It is your responsibility to routinely monitor and manage your EagleMail.

  Claiming your Eagle Access account will also enable you to access other FGCU systems such as Gulfline, which enables you to view your personal records and complete student-related tasks such as registering for University Housing.

  **How do I claim my account?**

  1. Go to www.fgcu.edu/myaccount
  2. To claim your account, you'll need the following information. Please make sure to enter this information as it appears on your admittance email.
     1. First Name
     2. Last Name
     3. UIN
  3. Please complete all of the steps to claim your account before exiting. If you do not complete all of the steps then you will need to contact the University Help Desk to have account claiming reset on your account.

  **What if I need help?**

  The University Help Desk is available to assist you with questions or concerns. You may reach the Help Desk at 239-590-1188 or helpdesk@fgcu.edu.
* Provide your Decision Reply and Submit your Admission Deposit!

  In order to accept your offer of admission, you must log into your Future Eagle Account and answer your Decision Reply question. On the Home tab of your Future Eagle Account, look at the right-hand column under "Forms."

  After accepting your offer of admission, you will be able to submit your $200 non-refundable deposit. To submit your Admission Deposit, go to your Home tab on your Future Eagle Account.  From there, look for "Payment Due" and select Submit Payment for $200.00 USD. Once you submit your deposit, we will hold a seat for you and you will move forward in the new student process. The deposit deadline for Fall is May 1st, however, we may accept deposits after the deadline until the term is full. The admission deposit is applicable to the term for which the student has been admitted and is non-refundable. The deposit will be forfeited if the student does not enroll in the deposited term. If a request to change terms is made after classes for the original term of admission have begun, a deposit for the new term must be paid.
* Register for Orientation

  All new students must attend **Eagle View Orientation (EVO)**. Directions on how to register for an orientation session for the Summer and Fall semesters will be sent out starting in February. We will begin sending the EVO email in October for students enrolling in the Spring semester. Register for EVO as soon as you can in order to have the best selection of courses. Orientation is held in late fall for the Spring term and throughout the summer for the Summer and Fall terms. **Get orientation details**
* Apply for Housing

  If you plan to live on campus, do not wait to **apply for housing**! We have three housing options: apartment-style living in North Lake or West Lake Villages or suite-style living in South Village, which features our First Year Residential Experience. Regardless of your preference, you must apply EARLY to get the best chance to secure your 1st choice. **Get details on housing**
* Establish your Residency

  All students must establish residency as a Florida Resident or non-Florida resident for tuition purposes.  **New students are considered non-Florida residents until proper documentation is received. Non-Florida residents also need to complete this process. Completing residency is essential for correct tuition billing, financial aid awarding, and merit scholarship eligibility.** Log into your Future Eagle account to complete the residency form. To complete your Residency Form, go to your Home screen.  In the right-hand column, you will find your Residency Form under "Forms."  Select the link, fill out the form, and submit it.  Note: if you indicate you are a Florida Resident, you will need to provide documentation to support your claim.
* Apply for Financial Aid/Scholarships

  March 1st is the FGCU priority deadline for the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) and the FGCU Foundation Scholarships. You can access the FAFSA by visiting our Financial Aid website - our federal school code is 032553. The Foundation Scholarships application closes on March 1st. We encourage you to apply annually by our priority deadline. **Get Scholarship and Financial Aid details**
