Sushmit Sharma Bhattarai, Ph.D., is a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Stock Development Department of Construction Management at Florida Gulf Coast University. His teaching and research interests include construction project management, digital construction technologies, infrastructure asset management, life-cycle decision-making, and smart, age-friendly built environments. His future research focuses on using BIM/VDC, reality capture, augmented reality, and stakeholder-informed decision frameworks to improve accessibility, wayfinding, safety, renovation planning, and long-term facility performance for older adults. He brings more than ten years of professional experience in heavy-civil and hydropower infrastructure.
