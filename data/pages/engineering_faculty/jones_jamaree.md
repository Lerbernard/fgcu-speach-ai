Jamaree Jones serves as an Academic Programs Coordinator II in the U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering at Florida Gulf Coast University. Prior to joining the college, he spent more than four years in FGCU Undergraduate Admissions, supporting prospective students and families throughout the admissions and enrollment process. Jamaree is a two-time FGCU graduate, earning both his bachelor's and master's degrees in Entrepreneurship from the Daveler & Kauanui School of Entrepreneurship. His own journey at FGCU shaped his belief in the transformative power of education and continues to inspire his commitment to helping students grow, discover their path, and make the most of their college experience.
