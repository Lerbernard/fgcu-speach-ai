Dr. Ali Ozdagli is an Assistant Professor of Civil Engineering at Florida Gulf Coast University. He earned Ph.D. degrees in Civil Engineering from Purdue University and Computer Science from Vanderbilt University, an M.S. in Civil Engineering from the University of Notre Dame, and a B.S. in Civil Engineering from Boğaziçi University. Prior to joining FGCU, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of New Mexico. His research lies at the intersection of structural engineering and computing, with interests in structural dynamics, structural health monitoring, real-time hybrid simulation, wireless and edge sensing, and machine learning for civil infrastructure. His work combines experimental research, sensing technologies, computational methods, and data-driven approaches to improve the monitoring and assessment of structural systems. He also actively mentors undergraduate and graduate students through research and project-based learning.

**Research and Teaching Interests**
Dr. Ozdagli's research focuses on structural dynamics, structural health monitoring, real-time hybrid simulation, low-cost wireless and edge sensing, and data-driven methods for civil infrastructure. His work combines experimental structural engineering with computing, embedded sensing, machine learning, and cyber-physical systems. He incorporates these areas into teaching through hands-on experiments, computational tools, real-world engineering examples, and undergraduate research experiences.

**Publications**
Dr. Ozdagli's research has been published in journals including Mechanical Systems and Signal Processing, Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering, Structural Control and Health Monitoring, Journal of Bridge Engineering, Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering, International Journal of Prognostics and Health Management, and Applied Artificial Intelligence. His work has also appeared in conference proceedings in structural dynamics, cyber-physical systems, structural health monitoring, and civil infrastructure engineering.
