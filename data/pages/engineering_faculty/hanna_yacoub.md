Dr. Yacoub Hanna joined Florida Gulf Coast University (FGCU) as a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Computing & Software Engineering in the fall of 2026. Previously, he served as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Florida International University (FIU), where he conducted research in the Advanced Wireless and Security (ADWISE) Lab. Dr. Hanna graduated from FIU in 2026 with a Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering. He also received his Master of Science in Computer Engineering from FIU in 2025 and his Bachelor of Science in 2020, graduating magna cum laude with a minor in Mathematical Sciences.

**Research and Teaching Interests**
Dr. Hanna’s research focuses on cybersecurity, PQC, and the security of critical infrastructure, with an emphasis on developing efficient security mechanisms for resource-constrained devices and cyber-physical systems. His research interests also include Internet of Things (IoT) security, smart grids, 5G, and satellite communications. He integrates his research into teaching through hands-on experiments and practical projects, engaging students in cybersecurity, cryptographic techniques, digital systems, and real-world security challenges.
