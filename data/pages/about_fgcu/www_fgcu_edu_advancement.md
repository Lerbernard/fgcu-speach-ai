## FGCU Foundation

The foundation advances Florida Gulf Coast University and its students by promoting the institution, securing philanthropy to support the needs of the university, managing and administering assets with a long-term perspective, and providing resources to the university for the benefit of its teaching, scholarship and public-service mission.
