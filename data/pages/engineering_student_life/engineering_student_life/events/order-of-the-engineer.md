# Order of the Engineer

The Order of the Engineer induction is held each Spring semester. The specific date for the upcoming semester is announced closer to the event.

The Order of the Engineer is a national organization that welcomes students into the engineering profession. During the induction ceremony, participants take an ethical oath, learn about the standards and responsibilities of being an engineer, and receive a ring as a symbol of their commitment to the field. It is a meaningful way to celebrate your path into the profession and connect with its values.
