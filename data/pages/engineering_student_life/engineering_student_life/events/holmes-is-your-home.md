# Holmes is Your Home

Holmes is Your Home takes place on August 19, 2026 from 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm on the Holmes Hall Lawn. It is held every Fall semester as the college's kick-off event.

Holmes is Your Home is the U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering's annual kick-off event for the school year. Whether you are an incoming or returning student, it is a great way to connect with the college: learn about WCE Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) and resources, lean into your competitive side with games and activities, and enjoy some free snacks. It is a great chance to mingle with students in your major, meet your faculty, and get excited for the new semester.
