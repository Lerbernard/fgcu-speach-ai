# Concrete Expo

Concrete Expo (Concrete Exposure Day) takes place on October 27, 2026 in the Cohen Student Union Ballroom. It is held each Fall semester.

Concrete Exposure Day brings together FGCU, the Florida Department of Transportation, and the Florida Concrete & Products Association to highlight the latest practices in the concrete industry. Students can explore real-world applications of their degree, see demonstrations, and hear from industry experts. The event also offers valuable networking opportunities. Professionals may receive professional-development hours for attending all sessions, with certificates provided at the conclusion of the last session.
