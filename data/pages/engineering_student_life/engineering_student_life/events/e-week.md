# Engineers Week (E-Week)

Engineers Week (E-Week) is held once a year. The 2026 E-Week dates are announced closer to the event.

Engineers Week is part of a national, annual celebration of engineering. Throughout the week, students can attend a series of events put on by student organizations and the college. E-Week lets students explore real-world applications of engineering, connect with guest speakers and industry professionals, and learn about different fields and career opportunities. Events can include networking sessions, student-organization activities, and fun traditions like the annual Students vs. Faculty Tug-of-War.
