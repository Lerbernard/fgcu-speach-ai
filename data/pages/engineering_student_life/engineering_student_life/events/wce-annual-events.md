# WCE Annual Events

Events students can attend during the year, as described by the college. Exact dates for the current year are in the WCE Events Calendar.

**Concrete Expo | Fall Semester**
Concrete Exposure Day brings together FGCU, the Florida Department of Transportation, and the Florida Concrete & Products Association to highlight the latest practices in the concrete industry. Students can explore real-world applications of their degree, see demonstrations, and hear from industry experts. The event also offers valuable networking opportunities.

**Eagle X | Fall & Spring Semesters**
Eagle X is a university-wide showcase where students share their experiences in internships, leadership, service-learning, and research. Presentations are judged by industry partners, and awards are given across several categories. It's an opportunity to celebrate student achievements, gain inspiration from peers, and see the many ways Eagles are making an impact.

**Engineers Week (E-Week) | Spring Semester**
Engineers Week is part of a national, annual celebration of engineering. Throughout the week, students can attend a series of events put on by student organizations and the college. E-Week allows students to explore real-world applications of engineering, connect with guest speakers and industry professionals, and learn about different fields and career opportunities. E-Week events can include networking sessions, student organization activities, and fun traditions like the annual Students vs. Faculty Tug-of-War. 2026 E-Week Events

**Hard Hat Ceremony | Fall & Spring Semesters**
The Hard Hat Ceremony is a tradition celebrating construction management seniors as they transition from students to professionals. Graduates receive a personalized blue hard hat and Stock Development CM polo, presented by industry partners who have supported their journey. The event also includes awards, a catered reception, and recognition of each senior's achievements.

**Holmes is Your Home | Fall Semester**
Holmes is Your Home is the college's annual kick-off event for the school year. Enjoy games, snacks, and giveaways while getting connected to student organizations and learning what WCE has to offer. It's a great way to get helpful resources, meet classmates and faculty, and get excited for the semester ahead.

**Order of the Engineer | Spring Semester**
The Order of the Engineer is a national organization that welcomes students into the engineering profession. During the induction ceremony, participants take an ethical oath, learn about the standards and responsibilities of being an engineer, and receive a ring as a symbol of their commitment to the field. It's a meaningful way to celebrate your path into the profession and connect with its values. Learn More

**Stock Development Day | Fall Semester**
Stock Development Day kicks off the semester for construction management students with a two-part event. First, the Associated Builders and Contractors (ABC) student chapter hosts a welcome back event. Later, executives from Stock Development share insights into the construction and development market in Southwest Florida, followed by an open Q&A where students can ask questions and connect directly with industry leaders.

**WCE Career Expo | Fall & Spring Semesters**
The WCE Career Expo is the college's biggest event for internships and job opportunities in engineering, computing, and construction. Students can network with employers, explore different fields and career paths, and practice valuable professional skills. Students are encouraged to come prepared with copies of their resume and to wear professional attire. Learn more

**WCE Day | Fall & Spring Semesters**
WCE Day is a college-wide celebration showcasing the achievements of our students, faculty, and staff. Seniors present the projects they've developed with industry partners, and the day concludes with an award ceremony recognizing Students of the Year, Hall of Fame inductees, and other awards.
