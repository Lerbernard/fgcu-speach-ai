# WCE Chapter One

WCE Chapter One takes place on August 28, 2026 from 11:00 am to 1:00 pm at Holmes Hall. It is held at the start of the Fall semester.

WCE Chapter One is for all incoming students majoring in bioengineering, civil engineering, computer science, construction management, environmental engineering, and software engineering. Stop by Holmes Hall to meet your peers, connect with resources, and put together a toolkit to help you succeed in your first semester.
