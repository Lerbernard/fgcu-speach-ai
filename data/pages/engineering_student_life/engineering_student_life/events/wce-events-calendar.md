# WCE Events Calendar

Official dates from the U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering events calendar. When another page gives a different date for one of these events, these dates are the current ones.

**Holmes is Your Home**
Holmes is Your Home — August 19, 2026 (2:00 pm - 4:00 pm). Location: Holmes Hall Lawn. Whether you're an incoming student or a returning one, Holmes is Your Home is a great way to connect with the U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering (WCE). Learn about WCE Registered Student Organizations (RSOs) and resources, lean into your competitive side with activities and games, and enjoy some free snacks! This is a great chance to mingle with students in our majors, meet your faculty, and get excited for the new semester.

**WCE Chapter One**
WCE Chapter One — August 28, 2026 (11:00 am - 1:00 pm). Location: Holmes Hall. This event is for all incoming students majoring in bioengineering, civil engineering, computer science, construction management, environmental engineering, and software engineering. Stop by Holmes Hall to meet your peers, connect with resources, and put together a toolkit to help you succeed in your first semester.

**Stock Development Day**
Stock Development Day — September 2, 2026 (4:30 pm - 5:45 pm). Start off the semester by connecting with the Associated Builders & Contractors (ABC) student chapter and hearing from industry leaders about the latest updates on the construction market in Southwest Florida.

**WCE Career Expo**
WCE Career Expo — October 6, 2026 (9:00 am - 12:00 pm). Location: Cohen Student Union Ballroom & 201. Whether you're looking for a job or internship in the fields of engineering, computing, and construction, or you just want to learn what companies are out there, stop by the WCE Career Expo! This year, we will also have a Resource Room. Explore advanced degree options and learn about alumni opportunities. All majors and years are encouraged to attend; no experience necessary. Don't forget to bring copies of your resume and prepare your elevator pitch! Stop by Career Development Services or an RSO career fair prep event to help you put your best foot forward.

**Concrete Expo**
Concrete Expo — October 27, 2026. Location: Cohen Student Union Ballroom. Concrete Exposure Day brings together FGCU, the Florida Department of Transportation, and the Florida Concrete & Products Association to highlight the latest practices in the concrete industry. Students can explore real-world applications of their degree, see demonstrations, and hear from industry experts. The event also offers valuable networking opportunities.

WCE Showcase — December 4, 2026. Location: Holmes Hall.
