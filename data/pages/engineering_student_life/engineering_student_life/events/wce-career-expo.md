# WCE Career Expo

The WCE Career Expo takes place on October 6, 2026 from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm in the Cohen Student Union Ballroom & 201. It is held each Fall and Spring semester.

The WCE Career Expo is the college's biggest event for internships and job opportunities in engineering, computing, and construction. Whether you are looking for a job or internship or just want to learn what companies are out there, stop by. This year there is also a Resource Room where you can explore advanced-degree options and alumni opportunities. All majors and years are encouraged to attend; no experience necessary. Bring copies of your resume and prepare your elevator pitch. You can visit Career Development Services or an RSO career-fair prep event to help you put your best foot forward.
