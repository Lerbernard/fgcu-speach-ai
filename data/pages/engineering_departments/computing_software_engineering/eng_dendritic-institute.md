# Dendritic: A Human-Centered AI and Data Science Institute

## Human-Centered AI for Education, Research, and Regional Impact

The Dendritic Institute for Human-Centered AI & Data Science is FGCU's hub for responsible artificial intelligence, data science, education, applied research, and regional innovation. As an academic sub-brand of the U.A. Whitaker College of Engineering and located at the Emergent Technologies Institute, Dendritic connects students, faculty, staff, industry, government, nonprofits, and community partners to help people and organizations understand, evaluate, and use AI responsibly, critically, and productively.

### What We Do

Dendritic advances human-centered AI through three connected branches.

AI Education

Prepares students, faculty, staff, professionals, and community members to understand and use AI responsibly, through workshops, trainings, webinars, micro-credentials, summer academies, and faculty development.

AI Excellence Hub

Helps organizations adopt AI responsibly and effectively through readiness assessments, strategy workshops, upskilling, applied projects, industry forums, and innovation partnerships.

AI Research Builder

Strengthens FGCU's capacity for AI and data science research through interdisciplinary collaboration, student research, grant development, publications, and applied prototypes.
